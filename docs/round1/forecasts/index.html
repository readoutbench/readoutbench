Every forecast is public here before the result is.

Pilot: 111 trials that had already reported

Before Round 1 we ran the same instructions on 111 company trials whose results came out between July and October 2026, after the models' training data. 73% met their primary goal. This is a retrospective test: it shows how the models behave, not how they will score on Round 1.

The models ranked the trials about as well as the registry model, or better, but forecast about 14 points too low. The best-estimate recipe takes its level from the registry model and its ranking from the models: it moves the models' average to the registry model's average, then averages the two. It uses no pilot outcomes, so it carries over to Round 1 unchanged. It was the best of 12 recipes compared on these trials, and its lead over the registry model is small: Round 1 is the real test.

Method

Every model got the round's instructions (SHA-256 741d8086…80cb5), unchanged, and batches of up to 10 trial records, with no web access. Gemini and GPT received them as one message through their APIs, with no system prompt and no tools; the Claude models ran as one agent per batch that could only read those two files and write its answers. A batch the round's checker refused, or that a provider failed to answer, was sent again, at most twice for a refused answer.

A trial meets its goal when the sponsor or investigators report the primary objective met. Labels come from the sponsor's own statements, with a source for every trial. Scores are Brier skill against the registry base rate, with intervals over trials; the first pass is on 31 March 2027, then 30 June and 31 December 2027.

The fingerprints (SHA-256) of the Claude Opus, Sonnet and Haiku 5.5 forecast files were published in the repository on 1 November 2026, before the round opened, while the files stayed private; the files shown here match them. The other three models were run after the round closed, and their files were published with their fingerprints on the dates shown.

The forecast files, run logs and code are in the public repository.
