# Trial NCT07260500
Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Phase II, Multicenter, Randomized, Open-label, Active-controlled Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety, and Pharmacokinetics of Polyethylene Glycol Recombinant Human Growth Hormone Injection in Short Children Born Small for Gestational Age (SGA)
- Short title: A Phase II Study of PEG-rhGH Injection for Short Children Born Small for Gestational Age: Efficacy, Safety, and Pharmacokinetics
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Changchun GeneScience Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Short Stature Children Born Small for Gestational Age (SGA)
- Interventions: PEG-rhGH Injection; hGH Injection
- Enrollment: 72
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-11-11; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-23; study completion (as registered): 2027-04-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Annual height velocity at Week 26 of treatment (time frame: 26 Weeks)

## Summary
This study will take place at multiple sites and is divided into three phases:an initial 4-week getting-to-know-you phase,a 26-week main phase where participants receive medication,and a 4-week follow-up phase.Participants will be divided into three groups:two will receive different doses of PEG-rhGH Injection,and the third will receive hGH Injection.The goal is to determine which medication works best.
