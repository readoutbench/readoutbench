## Trial
- Title: Compare the Safety and Efficacy of 177Lu-DOTATATE and Octreotide LAR in Unresectable or Metastatic, Progressive, Well-differentiated(G1 and G2) and SSTR-positive Adult GEP-NEN

- Sponsor: HTA Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: NETS Ga68 Lu177
- Interventions: 177Lu-DOTATATE injection; Octreotide LAR (Long-acting release)

- Start: 2023-12-12; primary completion (as registered): 2026-11-24; study completion (as registered): 2027-02-24

## Primary outcome(s)

   Compare the 18-month PFS rate of NET patients between 177Lu-DOTATATE Injection and long-acting octreotide

## Summary
The study is to evaluate the sstr antagonists, 68Ga-DOTATATE and 177Lu-DOTATATE,as a pair of diagnostic/therapeuticradiopharmaceuticals(theranostics)in patients with NETS
