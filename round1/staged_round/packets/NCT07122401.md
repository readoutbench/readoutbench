## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Double-dummy, Phase III Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of MT1013 Versus Cinacalcet as Active Control in Secondary Hyperparathyroidism Patients on Maintenance Dialysis
- Short title: Study of MT1013 for the Treatment of Patients With Secondary Hyperparathyroidism
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Shaanxi Micot Pharmaceutical Technology Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Secondary Hyperparathyroidism, Chronic Kidney Disease
- Interventions: MT1013; Cinacalcet
- Enrollment: 424
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-09-30; primary completion (as registered): 2026-11-26; study completion (as registered): 2026-11-26

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Percentage of participants with > 50% reduction from baseline in mean intact parathyroid (iPTH) (time frame: EAP（Week 22-27）)
2. Percentage of participants with > 30% reduction from baseline in mean iPTH (time frame: EAP（Week 22-27）)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Change From Baseline in Bone Mineral Density
- Change from baseline in serum calcium
- Change From Baseline in serum phosphorus
- Safety and Tolerance

## Summary
MT1013 is a first-in-class dual-target agonist. This study is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, double-dummy phase III clinical study being conducetd to evaluate the efficacy and safety of MT1013 compared with active control cinacalcet in secondary hyperparathyroidism patients on maintenance dialysis. Subjects will be 1:1 randomized to receive MT1013 or cinacalcet for 26 weeks. Evaluations of iPTH, Ca, P, BMD, and biomarkers will be done across the study period, to compare efficacy and safety of MT1013 to cinacalcet.
