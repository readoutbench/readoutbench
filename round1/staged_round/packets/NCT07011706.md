## Eligibility (abridged)

Topical antibiotics within 1 week prior the baseline visit (W0D1) 4. Topical medicated treatment that could affect atopic dermatitis should be prohibited for at least 2 weeks prior to baseline visit. Example: topical corticosteroids, crisaborole, calcineurin inhibitors, ruxolitinib, roflumilast, tars, antimicrobials, medical devices, and bleach baths. 5.
