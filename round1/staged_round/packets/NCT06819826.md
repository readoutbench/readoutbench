## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Phase III Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of SC0062 Capsule in Patients with IgA Nephropathy and Proteinuria (SUCCESS-1)
- Short title: A Study of SC0062 Capsule for the Treatment of IgA Nephropathy with Proteinuria
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Biocity Biopharmaceutics Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: IgA Nephropathy (IgAN)
- Interventions: SC0062 strength 10mg; Placebo matched to SC0062
- Enrollment: 360
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-02-14; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-31; study completion (as registered): 2028-12-31

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Change from baseline in 24-hour urine protein-to-creatinine ratio (24h-UPCR) at 36 weeks (time frame: Baseline, 36 weeks)
   UPCR is measured from the 24-hour urine collection samples.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Change from baseline in estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) at 108 weeks
- The total slope of estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) from baseline to 108 weeks

## Summary
This is a multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of SC0062 capsule compared to placebo in patients with IgA nephropathy in the presence of proteinuria. The participants must have a high risk of disease progression, despite of stable use of the maximum tolerated labelled or optimized dose of RAASi and/or SGLT2i for at least 12 weeks prior to randomization.
