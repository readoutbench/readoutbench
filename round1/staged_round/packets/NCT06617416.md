## Trial
- Title: A Randomized, Double-blind, Multicenter Phase III Study to Evaluate the Consolidation Therapy of AK104 Versus Sugemalimab in Patients With Unresectable Locally Advanced Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer Who Have Not Progressed After Concurrent or Sequential Chemoradiotherapy
- Short title: A Study of AK104 in Subjects With Unresectable Locally Advanced NSCLC
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Akeso
- Conditions: NSCLC (Non-small Cell Lung Cancer)
- Interventions: Cadonilimab (AK104); Sugemalimab
- Enrollment: 560
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2024-11-12; primary completion (as registered): 2026-10-28; study completion (as registered): 2028-12-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Progression-free survival (PFS) assessed by INV (time frame: 3 years)
   PFS is defined as the time from randomization until the first documentation of disease progression or death due to any cause, whichever occurs first (based on RECIST Version 1.1).

## Main secondary outcomes
- OS
- 6-month PFS rate assessed by INV
- AEs

## Summary
This study compares the efficacy and safety of AK104 versus Sugemalimab as consolidation therapy in patients with unresectable, locally advanced NSCLC who have not progressed following concurrent or sequential chemoradiotherapy.
