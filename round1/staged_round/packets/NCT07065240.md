## Trial
- Title: A Randomized, Parallel-Group, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Monotherapy Study of the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of SPT-300 in Adults With Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), With or Without Anxious Distress

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Change From Baseline to Day 42 in Hamilton Depression Rating Scale-17 (HAM-D-17) Total Score (time frame: Baseline (Day 0) to Day 42)
   HAM-D-17 is a 17-item, clinician-administered rating scale to assess the severity of symptoms in participants diagnosed with depression. ## Main secondary outcomes
- Change From Baseline to Day 42 in Clinical Global Impression - Severity (CGI-S)

## Summary
This is a randomized, parallel-group, double-blind, placebo-controlled, monotherapy study to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of SPT-300 (GlyphAllo) in adults with major depressive disorder (MDD), with or without anxious distress.
