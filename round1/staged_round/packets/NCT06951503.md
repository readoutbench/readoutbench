## Trial
- Title: A Randomized, Controlled, Multicenter Phase III Clinical Study of AK112 Combined With Chemotherapy Versus Bevacizumab Combined With Chemotherapy in First-line Metastatic Colorectal Cancer
- Short title: AK112 and Chemotherapy in First-line Metastatic Colorectal Cancer
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Akeso
- Conditions: Colorectal Adenocarcinoma
- Interventions: AK112; Oxaliplatin; Irinotecan; Leucovorin and 5-FU; Bevacizumab
- Enrollment: 560
- Sites: 2 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-05-27; primary completion (as registered): 2027-01-13; study completion (as registered): 2029-01-07

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Progression-free survival (PFS) assessed by blinded independent central review (BICR) (time frame: Up to approximately 3 years)
   PFS is defined as the time from randomization to the first documented progressive disease (PD) or death due to any cause, whichever occurs first (based on RECIST 1.1 criteria).

## Main secondary outcomes
- Overall survival (OS)
- Progression-free survival (PFS) assessed by investigator
- Objective Response Rate (ORR)
- Duration of Response (DoR)
- Disease control rate (DCR)

## Summary
This trial is a Phase III study. The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of AK112 and chemotherapy versus bevacizumab and chemotherapy for the first-line treatment of metastatic colorectal cancer.
