## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Multicenter Randomized Controlled Open-label Study of Autologous Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocytes (GT101 Injection) in Patients With Recurrent or Metastatic Cervical Cancer
- Short title: Autologous Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocytes (GT101 Injection) in Patients With Recurrent or Metastatic Cervical Cancer
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Grit Biotechnology
- Conditions: Adult
- Interventions: GT101 injection; Gemcitabine injection
- Enrollment: 83
- Sites: 24 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2024-04-02; primary completion (as registered): 2027-01-31; study completion (as registered): 2027-01-31

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Progression Free Survival (time frame: 3 years)
   Defined as time from first study treatment until disease progression or death from any cause, whichever occurs first.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Duration of Response
- Number of participants with treatment-related adverse events as assessed by CTCAE v5.0

## Summary
This is a Phase II, multicenter, open-label, randomized, parallel group, treatment study to assess the efficacy and safety of Autologous Tumor Infiltrating Lymphocytes (GT101 injection) compared with Gemcitabine in participants with recurrent or metastatic cervical cancer.
