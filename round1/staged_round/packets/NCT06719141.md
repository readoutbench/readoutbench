## Trial
- Title: A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multicenter Study to Investigate the Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of LP352 in the Treatment of Seizures in Children and Adults With Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathies
- Short title: A Study to Investigate LP352 in Children and Adults With Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathies (DEE)
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Longboard Pharmaceuticals
- Conditions: Developmental and Epileptic Encephalopathy
- Interventions: LP352; Placebo
- Enrollment: 367
- Sites: 116 in 17 countries (Australia, Belgium, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Latvia, Mexico, Netherlands, ...)
- Start: 2024-11-11; primary completion (as registered): 2026-10-31; study completion (as registered): 2026-11-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Frequency Percent Change in Countable Motor Seizures During Treatment Compared to Baseline (time frame: Baseline and up to 15 Weeks)
   The percent change from Baseline in countable motor seizure frequency during Treatment will be calculated as countable motor seizure frequency during Treatment minus countable motor seizure frequency during Screening and divided by seizure frequency during Screening and multiplied by 100 where each seizure frequency will be based on number of seizures.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Safety and Tolerability of LP352
- Percentage of participants with ≥ 50% Reduction in countable motor seizures during the Treatment compared to Baseline
- Frequency Percent Change in Countable Motor Seizures during Maintenance compared to Baseline

## Summary
This (DEEp OCEAN Study) is a double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled, multicenter study to investigate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of LP352 in the treatment of seizures in children and adults with DEE. The study consists of 3 main phases: Screening, Titration period, Maintenance period, followed by a Taper period and Follow-Up. The total duration of the study will be approximately 24 months.
