## Summary
This is a Phase 2b, global, multicenter, sequential, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel group, dose-ranging study in participants with moderate to severe hidradenitis suppurativa. The purpose of the main study is to assess the efficacy and safety of brivekimig in a dose-ranging study of participants with moderate to severe HS. Study details include: The study duration (per participant) will be up to approximately 164 weeks for participants transitioning into the long-term extension (LTE) period and will be up to approximately 60 weeks for participants not transitioning into the LTE period. The randomized treatment duration will be up to approximately 152 weeks

## Eligibility (abridged)
