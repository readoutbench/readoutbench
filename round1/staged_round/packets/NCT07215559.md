## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Parallel-Group, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Study to Investigate Weight Reduction With Macupatide and Eloralintide, Alone or in Combination, in Adult Participants With Obesity or Overweight and With Type 2 Diabetes
- Short title: A Study of Macupatide (LY3532226) and Eloralintide (LY3841136), Alone or in Combination, in Adults With Obesity or Overweight and With Type 2 Diabetes
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Eli Lilly and Company
- Conditions: Obesity; Overweight; Diabetes Mellitus, Type 2
- Interventions: Macupatide; Eloralintide; Macupatide Placebo; Eloralintide Placebo
- Enrollment: 200
- Sites: 42 in 4 countries (Argentina, Australia, Puerto Rico, United States)
- Start: 2025-10-16; primary completion (as registered): 2027-03; study completion (as registered): 2027-05

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Percent Change from Baseline in Body Weight (time frame: Baseline, Week 32)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Change from Baseline in Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)
- Change from Baseline in Body Mass Index (BMI)
- Change from Baseline in Fasting Glucose

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to investigate weight reduction with macupatide and eloralintide, alone or in combination, in adult participants with obesity or overweight and with type 2 diabetes. Participation in the study will last about 48 weeks.
