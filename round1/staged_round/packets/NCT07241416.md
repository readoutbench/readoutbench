## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Open-Label, Positive-Controlled Phase III Study of Rezvilutamide Combined With Androgen Deprivation Therapy (ADT) Versus Enzalutamide Combined With ADT for Treating Low-volume Metastatic Hormone Sensitive Prostate Cancer (mHSPC)
- Short title: A Phase III Clinical Study of Rezvilutamide in Patients With Metastatic Hormone-Sensitive Prostate Cancer
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Jiangsu HengRui Medicine Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Metastatic Hormone-sensitive Prostate Cancer (mHSPC)
- Interventions: Rezvilutamide Tablets; Enzalutamide Soft Capsules
- Enrollment: 206
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-12-05; primary completion (as registered): 2027-03; study completion (as registered): 2028-09

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Prostate Specific Antigen (PSA) undetectable rate based on central laboratory. (time frame: 6 months after randomization.)
   Defined as the proportion of subjects with a total PSA level below 0.2 ng/mL in central laboratory testing within 6 months after randomization.

## Main secondary outcomes
- PSA response rate based on local laboratory.

- Objective response rate (ORR).

- Time to PSA progression based on local laboratory.
- Time to castration-resistant prostate cancer (CRPC).

## Summary
This study aims to compare the efficacy and safety of rezvilutamide with enzalutamide in the treatment of patients with low-volume metastatic hormone sensitive prostate cancer.
