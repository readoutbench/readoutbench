## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Parallel-group Phase II Clinical Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of SHR-1139 Injection in Patients With Moderate to Severe Plaque Psoriasis
- Short title: Phase II Clinical Trial of SHR-1139 Injection in Patients With Psoriasis
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Guangdong Hengrui Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd
- Conditions: Psoriasis
- Interventions: The SHR-1139 injection; placebo
- Enrollment: 60
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2026-08-04; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-31; study completion (as registered): 2027-10-31

## Primary outcome(s)
1. The proportion of participants who achieved a 100% improvement in the Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) score compared to the baseline by the 12th week (PASI 100). (time frame: 12th week)

## Main secondary outcomes
- The proportion of participants who achieved a minimum improvement of at least 75% in PASI compared to the baseline (PASI 75) in the 12th week;
- The proportion of participants who achieved a minimum improvement of at least 90% in PASI compared to the baseline (PASI 90) in the 12th week
- The proportion of participants who achieved a sPGA response of 0 or 1 (sPGA 0/1) as assessed by the static physician in the 12th week
- The proportion of participants who achieved a sPGA response of 0 (sPGA 0) in the 12th week

## Summary
This study is a randomized, double-blind, multi-center, parallel, phase II clinical trial, aiming to evaluate the efficacy and safety of SHR-1139 injection for adult patients with psoriasis. The trial includes a 4-week screening period, a 52-week treatment period, and a 12-week safety follow-up period. Approximately 60 patients with psoriasis are expected to participate.
