## Trial
- Title: A Multi-center, Randomized, Double-blind, Active Controlled, Parallel Groups, Phase II Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of HRS-8427 in the Treatment of Adults With Hospital-acquired Bacterial Pneumonia (HABP) or Ventilator-associated Bacterial Pneumonia (VABP)
- Short title: A Trial of HRS-8427 in the Treatment of Adults With Bacterial Pneumonia
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Jiangsu HengRui Medicine Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Hospital-acquired Bacterial Pneumonia (HABP); Ventilator-associated Bacterial Pneumonia (VABP)
- Interventions: HRS-8427; Meropenem
- Enrollment: 120
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-04-10; primary completion (as registered): 2026-09-22; study completion (as registered): 2026-10-09

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of HRS -8427 in patients with HABP/VABP.
