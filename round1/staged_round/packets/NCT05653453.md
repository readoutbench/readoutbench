## Trial
- Title: Clinical Study of Tumor Treating Fields Combined with Gemcitabine and Albumin-bound Paclitaxel in the First-line Treatment of Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Jiangsu Healthy Life Innovation Medical Technology Co., Ltd
- Conditions: Locally Advanced Pancreatic Cancer
- Interventions: Tumor treating fields combined with Gemcitabine hydrochloride and albumin binding paclitaxel; Gemcitabine hydrochloride and albumin binding paclitaxel
- Enrollment: 512
- Sites: 5 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2022-12-20; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-25; study completion (as registered): 2027-09-28

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Overall survival (OS) (time frame: up to 12 months after the last study treatment)
   From date of enrollment until the date of death from any cause

## Main secondary outcomes
- Number of participants with adverse events (AEs)
- Progression-free survival (PFS)
- 12-month OS rate
- Progression Free Survival rate at 6 months

## Summary
The Objectives of this clinical trial is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of tumor treating fields combined with gemcitabine and albumin bound paclitaxel in the treatment of locally advanced pancreatic cancer.
