# Trial NCT07215273
Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind Study of Safety and Efficacy of EL219 (Turletricin) Versus Liposomal Amphotericin B or Voriconazole for Early Antifungal Therapy of Invasive Mould Infections (TREAT-1)
- Short title: Phase 2 EL219 Versus Liposomal Amphotericin B or Voriconazole for Early Antifungal Therapy
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Elion Therapeutics, Inc.
- Conditions: Invasive Mould Infection
- Interventions: EL219; Active Comparator- IV Antifungal (LAmB or voriconazole)
- Enrollment: 60
- Sites: 30 in 6 countries (Belgium, Canada, France, Italy, Spain, United States)
- Start: 2026-02-13; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12; study completion (as registered): 2026-12

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Primary Outcome/Measure All-Cause Mortality (time frame: From enrollment to Day 21)
   All-cause mortality at Day 21 (plus or minus 3 days) in the Intent-to-Treat (ITT) analysis set.
2. Primary Outcome/Measure SAE TEAE (time frame: From enrollment to the end of treatment at Day 42)
   During the blinded portion of the study, serious adverse events and treatment-emergent adverse events categorized in a tiered approach in the Safety analysis set. Tier 1 TEAEs include renal, electrolyte, hepatic, infusion-related reactions, photophobia and photosensitivity. Tier 2 includes all other TEAEs.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Secondary Outcome Measure-EAT Success
- Secondary Outcome Measure-Overall Success
- Secondary Outcome Measure-Participant is Alive

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to determine if EL219 is safe and effective compared to liposomal amphotericin B (LAmB) or voricanozole for early treatment of invasive mould infections

## Detailed description (registry)
A Phase 2, multicenter, randomized, double-blind Study of Safety and Efficacy of EL219 versus Comparator (LAmB or voriconazole) for early antifungal therapy of suspected or confirmed Invasive Mould Infections (IMI)
