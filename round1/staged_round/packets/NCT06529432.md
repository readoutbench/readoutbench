## Trial
- Title: Efficacy, Safety and Pharmacokinetics of Bupivacaine Liposome Injection for Paravertebral Nerve Block in the Treatment of Postoperative Pain After Thoracoscopic Surgery: a Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Dose-finding, Positive Control, Phase Ⅱ Clinical Trial
- Short title: A Study of Bupivacaine Liposome Injection in the Treatment of Pain After Thoracoscopic Surgery
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Jiangsu HengRui Medicine Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Local Analgesia Via Nerve Block
- Interventions: Bupivacaine Liposome Injection; Bupivacaine Hydrochloride Injection
- Enrollment: 96
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2026-03-03; primary completion (as registered): 2026-11; study completion (as registered): 2026-11

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Area under the time curve of pain intensity during exercise (or cough) for 72 hours after administration (time frame: 0 minute to 72 hours after administration)

- The duration of the first pain intensity score ≥4 points at rest after anesthesia recovery

## Summary
The objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of bupivacaine liposomes for paravertebral nerve block in the treatment of thoracoscopic postoperative pain, and to evaluate the relevant human pharmacokinetics.
