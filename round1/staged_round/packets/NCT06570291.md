## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Controlled Phase III Trial of Allogenic Adipose Tissue-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells (AlloJoin®) Therapy for Knee Osteoarthritis.
- Short title: Allogenic Adipose-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells for the Treatment of Knee Osteoarthritis
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Wuxi Cellular Biopharmaceutical Group Ltd.
- Conditions: Knee Osteoarthritis
- Interventions: Mesenchymal Stem Cells; Sodium Hyaluronate Injection
- Enrollment: 520
- Sites: 2 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-02-28; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12; study completion (as registered): 2028-12

## Summary
Evaluate the efficacy and safety of Allogenic Adipose Tissue-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells Therapy
