## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Phase II Clinical Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of SHR0302 Tablets as Single Therapy or in Combination With SHR0302 Base Gel in the Treatment of Patients With Non-segmental Vitiligo
- Short title: A Trial of SHR0302 Tablets and SHR0302 Base Gel in Patients With Non-segmental Vitiligo
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Jiangsu HengRui Medicine Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Non-segmental Vitiligo
- Interventions: SHR0302 Tablets; SHR0302 Base Gel; SHR0302 Placebo Tablets; SHR0302 Base Placebo Gel
- Enrollment: 176
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-12-05; primary completion (as registered): 2027-03; study completion (as registered): 2027-09

## Primary outcome(s)
1. The percentage change in the facial vitiligo area score index (F-VASI) from the baseline. (time frame: At Week 24.)

## Main secondary outcomes
- The percentage change in F-VASI compared to the baseline.
- The percentage change in the total body vitiligo area score index (T-VASI) compared to the baseline.
- The proportion of subjects who achieved an improvement of at least 50%/75%/90% compared to the baseline on the F-VASI (F-VASI 50/75/90 response).
- The proportion of subjects who achieved at least a 50%/75%/90% improvement in T-VASI compared to the baseline (T-VASI 50/75/90 response).
- The absolute change in facial vitiligo area (F-BSA) compared to the baseline.
- The percentage change in facial vitiligo area (F-BSA) compared to the baseline.

## Summary
The study is being conducted to evaluate the efficacy, and safety of SHR0302 tablets as single therapy or in combination with SHR0302 Base gel for patients with non-segmental vitiligo.
