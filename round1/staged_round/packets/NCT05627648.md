Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled, Double-Blind, Parallel-Group Confirmatory Study For Intracordal Administration Of KP-100LI In Patients With Vocal Fold Scar
- Short title: Phase 3 Study of KP-100LI in Subjects With Vocal Fold Scar
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Kringle Pharma, Inc.
- Conditions: Vocal Fold Scar
- Interventions: KP-100LI; Placebo
- Enrollment: 62
- Sites: 8 in 1 countries (Japan)
- Start: 2022-12-01; primary completion (as registered): 2026-07-31; study completion (as registered): 2027-02-28

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Improvement rate in Voice Handicap Index-10 (VHI-10) score at 24 weeks of the observational period (time frame: 24 weeks)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Improvement rate in VHI-10 score from just before the first administration ("baseline" at 0 week) to 4, 8, and 12 weeks of the observational period
- Changes in VHI-10 score from baseline to 4, 8, 12, and 24 weeks of the observational period
- Changes in Normalized Mucosal Wave Amplitude (NMWA) from baseline to on 4, 8, 12, and 24 weeks of the observational period
- Changes in GRBAS (Grade, Roughness, Breathiness, Asthenia, Strain) scale from baseline to on 4, 8, 12, and 24 weeks of the observational period
- Evaluation of adverse events

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to confirm the efficacy and safety of KP-100LI, a intracordal formulation containing recombinant human dHGF as the active pharmaceutical ingredient, for voice function improvement therapy in patients with vocal fold scar (including vocal fold sulcus).

## Eligibility (abridged)
Inclusion Criteria: 1. 18 years to 75 years 2. Presence of bilateral vocal fold scar or sulcus diagnosed 3. No other vocal lesion or vocal movement disorder 4. Voice Handicap Index-10 (VHI-10) score of 11 or higher 5. No laryngoplasty, collagen or lipid infusion to vocal cord, removal scar, fascial graft, or administration of steroid or hyaluronic acid to vocal cord Exclusion Criteria: 1. No movement disorders of the vocal fold including paralysis 2. Airway disease caused by burn 3. History of malignant tumor 4. History of allergy to local anesthesia agent 5. With impaired coagulation-fibrinolysis, or taking anticoagulant or antiplatelet drugs 6. Serious concomitant disease 7. Pregnant (including suspected), nursing, wishing to become pregnant, or unable to prevent conception during the trial period using contraceptive methods
