## Trial
- Title: An Open-label, Randomized, Multi-center, Parallel, Phase III Clinical Trial to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Adjuvant Immuncell-LC Therapy Combined With Gemcitabine Versus Adjuvant Gemcitabine Single Therapy After Resection in Patients With Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma
- Short title: Safety and Efficacy of Immuncell-LC With Gemcitabine in Resectable Pancreatic Cancer
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: GC Cell Corporation
- Conditions: Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma
- Interventions: Immuncell-LC; Gemcitabine
- Enrollment: 408
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (South Korea)
- Start: 2021-09-07; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12; study completion (as registered): 2027-06

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Recurrence free survival (RFS) by independent review (time frame: Up to approximately 36 months after Last Patient In)
   Recurrence free survival is defined as the time from randomization to the date of the recurrence confirmed by independent reviewer

## Main secondary outcomes
- Overall survival (OS)
- Recurrence free survival (RFS) by investigator
- Carbohydrate antigen 19-9 level
- Quality of Life (QoL) EORTC QLQ-C30
- Quality of Life (QoL) EORTC QLQ-PAN26

## Summary
PURPOSE: This phase III clinical trial evaluates the efficacy and safety of adjuvant Immuncell-LC therapy combined with gemcitabine versus adjuvant gemcitabine single therapy after R0 or R1 resection in patients with pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma.

## Detailed description (registry)
Open-label, Randomized, Multi-center, Parallel, Phase III Clinical Trial
