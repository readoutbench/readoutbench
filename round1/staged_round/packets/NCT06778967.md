## Trial
- Title: A Multicenter, Randomized, Active-controlled Phase III Clinical Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of GZR18 Injection in Chinese Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Poor Blood Glucose Control by Metformin Monotherapy or in Combination With Sodium-dependent Glucose Transporter 2 (SGLT2) Inhibitors or Sulfonylureas

- Acronym: Optimum 2
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Gan & Lee Pharmaceuticals.
- Conditions: Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM)
- Interventions: GZR18; semaglutide
- Enrollment: 1100

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Change from Baseline in Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c)(%) (time frame: Baseline, Week 44)

## Main secondary outcomes

- Proportion of subjects with salvage therapy.

## Summary
The main purpose of this study is to investigate the Efficacy and Safety of GZR18 Injection in Chinese Subjects with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus and Poor Blood Glucose Control by Metformin Monotherapy or in Combination with Sodium-dependent Glucose Transporter 2 (SGLT2) Inhibitors or Sulfonylureas

## Eligibility (abridged)
