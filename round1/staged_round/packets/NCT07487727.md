## Trial
- Title: A Phase 3, Multi-center, Randomized, Open-Label, Active-Controlled, Efficacy and Safety Study of AND017 to Treat Anemia in Non-Dialysis-Dependent Chronic Kidney Disease (NDD-CKD) Patients
- Short title: A Study of AND017 to Evaluate Efficacy and Safety in Patients With Anemia Due to Non-Dialysis-Dependent Chronic Kidney Disease (NDD-CKD)
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Kind Pharmaceuticals LLC
- Conditions: Anemia Due to Chronic Kidney Disease
- Interventions: AND017; ESA
- Enrollment: 240
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2025-12-03; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-01; study completion (as registered): 2027-07-01

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Evaluate the efficacy of AND017 compared with the active control in maintaining Hemoglobin (Hb) levels in patients with anemia due to CKD (time frame: From Week 23 to Week 27)
   The mean Hb levels averaged over Week 23-27

## Main secondary outcomes
- The percentage of responders
- Percentage of participants that maintained Hb level over target lower limit
- Maintenance of Hb within 10.0-12.0 g/dL after initial achievement ≥10.0 g/dL during the entire study treatment period
- Incidence of extreme Hb levels of ≥13.0 g/dL or <7.5 g/dL during the entire study treatment period
- Incidence of excessive erythropoiesis
- The cumulative incidence of Hb non-response

## Summary
This is a Phase III, randomized, open-label, active-controlled study to evaluate the safety and efficacy of AND017 in non-dialysis-dependent (NDD)-CKD patients compared with the active control, ESA treatment
