Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Multicenter, Pharmacokinetic Equivalence Clinical Trial of QL2107 (Keytruda® Biosimilar Candidate) in Comparison With Keytruda® (Pembrolizumab) for Adjuvant Therapy to Demonstrate Pharmacokinetic Similarity in Subjects With Resected Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer
- Short title: Pharmacokinetic Study of QL2107 Versus Keytruda® for Adjuvant Therapy of Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC)
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: Qilu Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Carcinoma, Non-Small-Cell Lung
- Interventions: QL2107; Keytruda®
- Enrollment: 146
- Sites: 84 in 13 countries (Bosnia and Herzegovina, China, Georgia, Greece, Hungary, India, Poland, Romania, Spain, Thailand, Turkey (Türkiye), Ukraine, ...)
- Start: 2025-11-21; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-31; study completion (as registered): 2027-11-15

## Primary outcome(s)
1. AUCtau,sd of QL2107 and Keytruda (time frame: At Cycle 1 (cycle length = 21 days)])
   Area under the concentration time curve for 1 dosing interval (tau = 21 days) after a single initial) dose (AUCtau,sd) of QL2107 and Keytruda® will be reported.
2. AUCtau,ss of QL2107 and Keytruda (time frame: At Cycle 7 (cycle length = 21 days)])
   Area under the concentration time curve for 1 dosing interval (tau = 21 days) at steady state (AUCtau,ss) of QL2107 and Keytruda® will be reported.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Cmax,sd of QL2107 and Keytruda
- Cmax,ss of QL2107 and Keytruda
- Ctrough of QL2107 and Keytruda
- Number of Participants With Antidrug Antibodies (ADAs) and Neutralizing Antibodies (NAbs)
- Number of Participants With Treatment-emergent Adverse Events (TEAEs)

## Summary
The primary purpose of this study is to demonstrate Pharmacokinetic similarity in exposure after the initial dose and at steady state of QL2107 compared with Keytruda.
