## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Randomized, Double-Blind, Multi-Center Study in Adult Subjects With Eosinophilic Esophagitis (EoE) to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Vonoprazan 20 mg Compared to Placebo After 12 Weeks and to Evaluate the Safety and Efficacy of Vonoprazan 20 mg Up to 24 Weeks
- Short title: A Study in Adult Participants With EoE to Evaluate Vonoprazan 20 mg Compared to Placebo After 12 Weeks and to Evaluate Vonoprazan 20 mg Up to 24 Weeks
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Phathom Pharmaceuticals, Inc.
- Conditions: Eosinophilic Esophagitis
- Interventions: Vonoprazan; Placebo
- Enrollment: 95
- Sites: 41 in 1 countries (United States)
- Start: 2025-10-30; primary completion (as registered): 2026-09-11; study completion (as registered): 2026-12

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Number of Participants at Week 12 Achieving Peak Esophageal Intraepithelial Eosinophil Count <15 eos/hpf. (time frame: Week 12)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Mean Change from Baseline to Week 12 in Dysphagia Days
- Mean Change from Baseline to Week 12 in EoE Endoscopic Reference Score (EREFS).
- Mean Change from Baseline to Week 12 in Peak Esophageal Intraepithelial Eosinophil Count.

## Summary
The primary objective of this study is to assess the efficacy of vonoprazan 20 mg once daily (QD) compared to placebo QD in the number of participants with a peak eosinophilic histologic response \<15 eosinophils per high-power field (eos/hpf) after 12 weeks of therapy.
