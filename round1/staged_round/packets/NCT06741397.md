## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2b Single Arm, Repeat Dose Study to Evaluate the Pharmacokinetic Profile, Safety, and Tolerability of A New Formulation of Cabotegravir LA Injected Intramuscularly Q4M in Adolescent and Adult Participants at Risk of HIV Acquisition
- Short title: A Study to Evaluate the Pharmacokinetics, Safety, and Tolerability of a New Formulation of Cabotegravir Long-Acting Administered Intramuscularly in a 4-month Dosing Interval (Q4M)
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: ViiV Healthcare
- Conditions: HIV Infections
- Interventions: CAB LA; New formulation of CAB LA
- Enrollment: 229
- Sites: 27 in 2 countries (Puerto Rico, United States)
- Start: 2024-12-20; primary completion (as registered): 2026-09-09; study completion (as registered): 2029-08-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. CAB trough concentrations for target of 1.05 microgram per milliliter (μg/mL) for men and 1.39 μg/mL for women (time frame: From Month 13 to Month 25)
   CAB trough concentrations are evaluated at or above the target of 1.05 μg/mL for men and 1.39 μg/mL for women, once steady state has been achieved, but no earlier than Month 13 of the study.

## Main secondary outcomes
- CAB trough concentrations for target of 0.672 μg/mL for men and 0.683 μg/mL for women
- CAB trough concentrations for target of 1.05 μg/mL for men and 1.39 μg/mL for women following loading dose
- CAB plasma concentrations
- CAB trough concentrations for target of 4x PA-IC90
- Percentage of participants with confirmed incident HIV infections
- Percentage of participants with Study Medication Satisfaction Questionnaire Status version (SMSQs) results
- and 5 more

## Summary
This study will evaluate the pharmacokinetics (PK), safety, and tolerability of a new formulation of Cabotegravir (CAB) dosed every 4-months (Q4M) for pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP) in participants at risk of HIV-1 acquisition.
