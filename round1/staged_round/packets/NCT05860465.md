## Trial
- Title: A Phase II/III Study of SPH4336 in Combination With Endocrine Therapy in the Treatment of HR-positive, HER2-negative Locally Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer That Progressed on CDK4/6 Inhibitor Combined With Endocrine Therapy
- Short title: Safety and Efficacy of SPH4336 in Combination With Endocrine Therapy in the Treatment of Locally Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer
- Phase: Phase 2, Phase 3
- Sponsor: Shanghai Pharmaceuticals Holding Co., Ltd
- Conditions: Locally Advanced or Metastatic Breast Cancer
- Interventions: SPH4336 Tablets; SPH4336 Tablets Placebo
- Enrollment: 254
- Sites: 22 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2023-09-08; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-01; study completion (as registered): 2026-12-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Objective response rate (time frame: Approximately 3years)
   tumor response will be evaluated according to the Response Evaluation Criteria Solid Tumors (RECIST) criteria version 1.1.
2. Progression-free survival (PFS) (time frame: Approximately 3years)
   from the start date of study treatment to the date of progression disease or death , whichever occurred first.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Cmax
- Tmax
- Disease control rate (DCR)
- Duration of remission (DOR)
- Overall Survival (OS)
- Safety and tolerability of the combination therapy since the start of any study treatment.

## Summary
This study evaluated the safety and efficacy of SPH4336 in combination with endocrine therapy in the treatment of locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer that progressed on CDK4/6 inhibitor combined with endocrine therapy.
