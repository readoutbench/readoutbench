## Trial

- Sponsor: Ocular Therapeutix, Inc.; collaborators: ['Fortrea', 'Duke University']
- Conditions: Neovascular Age-related Macular Degeneration
- Interventions: OTX-TKI; Aflibercept; Aflibercept
- Enrollment: 825
- Sites: 97 in 4 countries (Argentina, Australia, Puerto Rico, United States)

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Best Corrected Visual Acuity (BCVA) (time frame: Week 48)
   Mean change in Best Corrected Visual Acuity (BCVA) from baseline at Week 48

## Summary
Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Intravitreal OTX-TKI (Axitinib Implant) in Subjects with Neovascular Age- Related Macular Degeneration

## Eligibility (abridged)
Inclusion Criteria: * Treatment naïve juxtafoveal and/or SFNV secondary to Neovascular Age- Related Macular Degeneration (nAMD) with leakage involving the fovea in the study eye prior to Screening, or have a diagnosis of Neovascular Age- Related Macular Degeneration (nAMD) in study eye within 3 months prior to screening and received up to 2 monthly injections of 2 mg aflibercept with last injection approximately 4 weeks prior to Screening * Have provided written consent Exclusion Criteria: * Monocular subject or Best Corrected Visual Acuity (BCVA) score of \<20 ETDRS letters or 20/400 in fellow eye at Screening * Have evidence of a scar, fibrosis, or atrophy of \>50% of the total lesion in the study eye.
