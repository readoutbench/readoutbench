## Trial
- Title: A 52-week, Randomized, Double-blind, Double-dummy, Parallel-group, Multi-centre, Non-inferiority Study to Investigate the Efficacy and Safety of Depemokimab Compared With Mepolizumab in Adults With Relapsing or Refractory Eosinophilic Granulomatosis With Polyangiitis (EGPA) Receiving Standard of Care (SoC) Therapy
- Short title: Efficacy and Safety of Depemokimab Compared With Mepolizumab in Adults With Relapsing or Refractory Eosinophilic Granulomatosis With Polyangiitis (EGPA)
- Acronym: OCEAN
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: GlaxoSmithKline
- Conditions: Eosinophilic Granulomatosis With Polyangiitis
- Interventions: Depemokimab; Mepolizumab; Placebo matching mepolizumab; Placebo matching depemokimab
- Enrollment: 163
- Sites: 63 in 18 countries (Argentina, Austria, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, Israel, Italy, Japan, Netherlands, Poland, ...)
- Start: 2022-07-14; primary completion (as registered): 2026-09-29; study completion (as registered): 2026-10-27

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Number of participants with remission (Birmingham Vasculitis Activity Score [BVAS] =0 and a dose of oral corticosteroid [OCS] less than or equal to [<=] 4 milligram [mg] per day) (time frame: Up to Week 52)
   Participants must be in remission at both Weeks 36 and 52.

## Main secondary outcomes
- Number of participants in each category of accrued duration of remission
- Number of participants with total accrued duration of remission
- Time to first EGPA relapse
- Number of participants receiving in each category of mean OCS dose during the last 4 weeks of study treatment period (Weeks 49 to 52)
- Number of participants achieving remission (BVAS = 0 and OCS <= 4 mg/day) within the first 24 weeks with continued remission until Week 52
- Number of participants achieving remission using the European League against Rheumatism (EULAR) definition (BVAS = 0 and OCS <=7.5 mg/day) at Weeks 36 and 52
- and 3 more

## Summary
This study aims to investigate the efficacy and safety of depemokimab compared with mepolizumab in adults with relapsing or refractory EGPA receiving SoC therapy.
