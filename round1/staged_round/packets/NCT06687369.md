## Trial
- Title: Randomized, Multicenter, Multinational, Double-Blind Study to Compare the Pharmacokinetics, Efficacy, Safety and Immunogenicity of MB12 (Proposed Pembrolizumab Biosimilar) Versus Keytruda® in Combination With Chemotherapy for the Treatment of Patients With Advanced Stage IV Non-Squamous Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer (NSCLC) (BENITO Study)
- Short title: A Study to Compare Pharmacokinetics, Efficacy, Safety, and Immunogenicity of MB12 (Proposed Pembrolizumab Biosimilar) to Keytruda® in Non-small Cell Lung Cancer (BENITO Study)
- Phase: Phase 3
- Sponsor: mAbxience Research S.L.
- Conditions: Non Squamous Non Small Cell Lung Cancer
- Interventions: MB12 (Proposed Pembrolizumab Biosimilar); EU-sourced Keytruda®; US-sourced Keytruda®; Pemetrexed; Carboplatin; Cisplatin
- Enrollment: 726
- Sites: 173 in 24 countries (Armenia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Brazil, Georgia, Greece, Italy, Japan, Jordan, Malaysia, Moldova, Panama, Philippines, ...)
- Start: 2024-12-30; primary completion (as registered): 2026-08-31; study completion (as registered): 2027-09

## Primary outcome(s)
1. To demonstrate the pharmacokinetic (PK) bioequivalence of MB12, EU-sourced Keytruda® and US-sourced Keytruda® in combination with chemotherapy (time frame: Week 1 - Week 24)
   Area under the concentration-time curve (AUC) between Cycle 1 and Cycle 2 (AUC from time 0 to 504 hours postdose \[AUC0-504\]. AUC at steady state (AUCss) between Cycle 7 and Cycle 8.
2. To demonstrate the efficacy equivalence of MB12 and Keytruda® in combination with chemotherapy administered as first-line treatment in patients with advanced/metastatic non-squamous NSCLC (any PD-L1 expression type). (time frame: Week 1 - Week 24)
   Objective response rate (ORR), up to and including 24 weeks (end of Cycle 8)

## Main secondary outcomes
- To assess the efficacy of MB12 as compared with Keytruda® based on other efficacy parameters and timepoints over the study period.
- To compare the PK profile based on other PK parameters and timepoints (not covered by the primary PK endpoints) of MB12 as compared with Keytruda® over the study period.
- To assess the safety and tolerability of MB12 as compared with Keytruda®
- To assess the immunogenicity of MB12 as compared with Keytruda®

## Summary
This is a randomized, multicenter, multinational, double-blind, integrated pharmacokinetics (PK) and efficacy similarity study to compare the PK, efficacy, safety, and immunogenicity of MB12 versus Keytruda® in combination with pemetrexed-platinum chemotherapy as first-line treatment in patients with metastatic non-squamous NSCLC.
