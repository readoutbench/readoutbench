# Trial NCT07548632
Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2b/3, Double-masked, Randomized, Vehicle-controlled, Multicenter Study Evaluating Licaminlimab in Dry Eye Disease Patients With a Specific TNFR1 Genotype
- Short title: A Clinical Study Evaluating Licaminlimab for Dry Eye Disease
- Acronym: PREDICT-1
- Phase: Phase 2, Phase 3
- Sponsor: Oculis
- Conditions: Dry Eye Disease (DED)
- Interventions: Artificial Tear Run-in; licaminlimab; Vehicle of licaminlimab
- Enrollment: 160
- Sites: 14 in 1 countries (United States)
- Start: 2026-04-16; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12; study completion (as registered): 2026-12

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Change from baseline in global ocular discomfort severity score at Day 29 in participants with DED and specific TNFR1 genotype (time frame: From Day 1 to Day 29)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Change from baseline in global ocular discomfort severity score at Day 29 in all participants with DED (independent of genotype).

## Summary
The primary objective of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of the topical ophthalmic administration of licaminlimab as compared to vehicle in participants with Dry Eye Disease and a specific genotype.

## Eligibility (abridged)
Key Inclusion Criteria: * Physician diagnosis of Dry Eye Disease in the past 6 months * Use of over-the-counter tears * Must agree to genotype testing Key Exclusion Criteria: \- Have a history or presence of any disorder or condition that may, in the opinion of the Investigator, likely interfere with the interpretation of the study results or participant safety.
