# Trial NCT05851443
Forecast the result of this randomized trial. The information below comes from its ClinicalTrials.gov record (snapshot 2026-10-29); no result is included.

## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Double-Blind, Randomized, Placebo-Controlled, Dose-Ranging, Efficacy and Safety Study of Povorcitinib in Participants With Inadequately Controlled Moderate to Severe Asthma
- Short title: A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety Study of Povorcitinib in Participants With Inadequately Controlled Moderate to Severe Asthma
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Incyte Corporation
- Conditions: Moderate to Severe Asthma
- Interventions: povorcitinib; placebo; ICS-LABA
- Enrollment: 247
- Sites: 82 in 7 countries (Argentina, Canada, Germany, Japan, Poland, Spain, United States)
- Start: 2023-07-11; primary completion (as registered): 2026-11-20; study completion (as registered): 2027-07-05

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Absolute change in pre-bronchodilator forced expiratory volume in the first 1 second (pre-BD FEV1) (time frame: Baseline ; Week 24)
   To assess the effect of povorcitinib on lung function (pre-BD FEV1) between baseline and week 24

## Main secondary outcomes
- Number of asthma exacerbations during the Placebo Controlled (PC) period
- Absolute change from baseline in pre-BD FEV1 at each visit
- Percent change from baseline in pre-BD FEV1 at each visit
- Absolute change from baseline in post-BD FEV1 at week 24
- Percent change from baseline in post-BD FEV1 at week 24
- Absolute change from baseline in pre-BD FVC at each visit
- and 1 more

## Summary
The study is being conducted to evaluate the effect of 3 dosing regimens of povorcitinib on pulmonary function
