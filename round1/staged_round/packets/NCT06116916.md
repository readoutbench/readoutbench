## Trial
- Title: A Phase 2, Multicenter, Randomized, Double-Masked, Parallel-Group Study to Assess the Efficacy and Safety of KHK4951, a Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor Receptor Inhibitor, in Patients With Diabetic Macular Edema
- Short title: Study to Assess the Efficacy & Safety of KHK4951 in Patients With Diabetic Macular Edema
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Kyowa Kirin Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Diabetic Macular Edema (DME)
- Interventions: KHK4951; Aflibercept Injection
- Enrollment: 185
- Sites: 87 in 4 countries (Australia, Japan, South Korea, United States)
- Start: 2024-01-09; primary completion (as registered): 2026-10-31; study completion (as registered): 2026-11-30

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Reduction of 15 or more letters in BCVA (Best corrected visual acuity) as measured by ETDRS visual acuity chart from baseline (time frame: For 36 weeks until the end of the trial)

## Main secondary outcomes
- The number of aflibercept IVT
- Change from baseline in SHRM as measured by SD-OCT
- Change from baseline in retinal morphology as measured by SD-OCT
- Change from baseline in leakage as measured by FA

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to evaluate efficacy and safety of KHK4951 eye drops in patients with DME.
