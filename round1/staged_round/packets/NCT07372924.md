## Trial
- Title: A Phase II, Multicenter, Randomized, Single-Blind, Dose Exploration, Positive Comparator Study to Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Tegileridine Fumarate Lnjection for Prolonged Mechanical Ventilation Abirritation in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
- Short title: A Trial of Tegileridine Fumarate Lnjection for Prolonged Mechanical Ventilation Abirritation in the Intensive Care Unit (ICU)
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Jiangsu HengRui Medicine Co., Ltd.
- Conditions: Abirritation in the ICU
- Interventions: Tegileridine Fumarate lnjection; Tegileridine Fumarate lnjection; Remifentanil Hydrochloride for Injection
- Enrollment: 121
- Sites: 2 in 1 countries (China)
- Start: 2026-02-24; primary completion (as registered): 2026-08-14; study completion (as registered): 2026-08-14

## Primary outcome(s)
1. Rate of abirritation success, abirritation success is defined as the percentage of time maintaining target abirritation in the entire drug administering time ≥ 70%. (time frame: within 3 days after administration of research drug)

## Main secondary outcomes
- Percentage of time maintaining target abirritation in the entire drug administering time.
- Percentage of subjects receiving rescue analgesic drug
- The total dosage of rescue analgesic drug
- Receiving the total dosage of sedation
- Nursing Scores
- Mechanical ventilation time.
- and 1 more

## Summary
The purpose of this study is to evaluate the efficacy and safety of Tegileridine Fumarate lnjection for prolonged abirritation(48h to 72h) during mechanical ventilation in the ICU.
