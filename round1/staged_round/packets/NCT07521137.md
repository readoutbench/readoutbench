## Trial
- Title: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled Phase 2a Study Evaluating the Safety, Tolerability, and Efficacy of OCF001 Intravaginal Gel in Women With Acute Vulvovaginal Candidiasis
- Short title: Biweekly Long-term Occidiofungin Study for Suppression of Mycotic Recurrence
- Acronym: BLOSSOM
- Phase: Phase 2
- Sponsor: Sano Chemicals Inc
- Conditions: Candidiasis, Vulvovaginal
- Interventions: OCF001 Intravaginal Gel; Placebo Gel
- Enrollment: 36
- Sites: 1 in 1 countries (United States)
- Start: 2026-06-04; primary completion (as registered): 2026-12-31; study completion (as registered): 2026-12-31

## Primary outcome(s)
1. To evaluate the clinical cure of OCF001 (drug product) to treat acute VVC infection following 7-day intravaginal dose of drug product (time frame: 7 days)
   Proportion of subjects achieving clinical cure at the Early Response, Test-of-Cure (TOC), and Sustained Response visits (Days 4, 8, and 25), defined as a VSS composite score, VSS total score = 0, and no requirement for additional systemic or topical antifungal therapy for VVC between baseline and TOC.
2. Evaluate the safety and local tolerability of OCF001 (drug product) intravaginal formulation in women with moderate-to-severe acute VVC. (time frame: 4 weeks)
   Incidence, severity, and relationship to study drug of treatment-emergent adverse events (TEAEs) from first dose through the end-of-study visits (including local vulvovaginal AEs).

Clinical diagnosis of symptomatic VVC or RVVC confirmed at baseline by positive KOH wet mount (i.e., when examined microscopically, vaginal secretions obtained by swab of the vaginal mucosa, placed on a slide and diluted with 10% room temperature potassium hydroxide (KOH) reveal filamentous hyphae/pseudohyphae and/or budding yeast cells). * VSS ≥ 5 * KOH or wet prep positive for budding yeast or pseudohyphae * Vaginal pH ≤ 4.5 3.
